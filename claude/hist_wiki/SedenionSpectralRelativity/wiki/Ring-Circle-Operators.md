# Ring–Circle Operators, slot by slot

**Job.** For each of the 16 slots of the sedenion, say what it is as a circle operator, which circles it deforms, what its bracketings and firing orders produce, and where the engine puts it. Full results and verdict: `VAPMIP/docs/wiki/Ring-Circle-Operators.md`; code and data: `VAPMIP/RingCircle/`. Everything here is `:CALCULATED` on this repo's own product (`emerger_spectrum.cd_mul`).

**Order.** Application order and traversal order are one order in two vocabularies; "direction" in the higher maths is nothing but the order arrived at (Cody, 2026-10-20). A firing order is therefore the order in which operators are applied *and* the order in which the tree is walked. Every "firing order" below is that one order.

Reading the columns. **Leaf path**: the engine's 31-node tree reads slot k as four L/R choices, the binary digits of k; each added axis is one binary choice. **Generator slots**: the powers of two (1, 2, 4, 8) whose product, in some bracketing and firing order, is ± e_k. **Bracketing → sign·(−1)^parity**: for a fixed bracketing the sign of the product is the parity of the firing order times the constant shown; two generators: +1 (they anticommute); three generators: (ab)c = +1, a(bc) = −1, i.e. (ab)c = −a(bc); four generators (slot 15): left comb +1, a((bc)d) +1, (a(bc))d −1, a(b(cd)) −1, and the balanced (ab)(cd) is order-dependent beyond parity. **Deform**: the circles whose rotation generators fail to anticommute with this slot's (Frobenius norm 4√2); the 42 Assessor pairs. **Euler circle**: the Euler factor of the slot's prime on σ = ½ is an exact circle with that centre and radius, turning at the rate ln p = ln 10 · log₁₀ p.

## What each slot makes available, equation-wise

- **Every slot:** Euler's identity in its own circle, e^(π e_k) = −1, e^((π/2) e_k) = e_k (the quarter turn: the **Sign** operator of Add : Scale : Sign, one per slot), L_k² = −I.
- **Every pair of slots:** conjugation u e_j u⁻¹ = cos 2θ e_j − sin 2θ (e_j e_k), exact in both bracketings: slot k rotates slot j in place by 2θ about the axis e_k (210 of 210 ordered pairs).
- **Pairs that anticommute (63 of 105):** mutually anticommuting slots close to a Clifford algebra: n of them give so(n+1) (1, 3, 6, 10 for n = 1..4).
- **Deforming pairs (42):** each of slots 1–7 deforms with six of slots 9–15, and each of 9–15 with six of 1–7. Such a pair doubles the operator ring (dimension 8 for {1,10}), and four slots containing three or more such pairs can leave the unit sphere (210 of 1,365 sets; norm ratio 0.626 … 1.289 in general).
- **Triples:** 35 triples that are lines of PG(3,2) give 6 distinct results over all orders and bracketings (associative); 168 give 8; 252 give 12.
- **The identity slot e₀:** the one point all 15 circles pass through (0_RB as e₀).
