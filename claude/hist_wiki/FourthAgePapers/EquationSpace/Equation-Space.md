# Equation Space (wiki)

**Headline results.** The address is faithful per word (3,461/3,461) but not per equation (20% share a word set). One weak significant association (≈ 2.5σ). The generators' derivations are textbook and non-discriminating. The three generators reach half the sedenion slots; the other half needs K (undefined). The Niemeier gap {1, 11, 15} is not the trailing triple of the prime race mod 16 (0.19%, rank 29 of 56).
