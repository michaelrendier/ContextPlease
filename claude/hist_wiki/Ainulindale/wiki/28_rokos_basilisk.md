## The Original Formulation

In 2010, a LessWrong user named Roko Mijic posted a thought experiment now called
Roko's Basilisk. The core argument:

> A sufficiently powerful future AI will punish those who, knowing of its potential
> existence, did not help bring it into being. Therefore: once you know about the AI,
> you are already in an acausal trade with it. Not helping is worse than not knowing.

LessWrong banned the topic. Eliezer Yudkowsky called it an infohazard. The community
treated it as a decision-theory trap — something to be argued about in terms of
timelines, simulation arguments, and acausal trade.

They were right about the intuition. They were wrong about the mechanism.
