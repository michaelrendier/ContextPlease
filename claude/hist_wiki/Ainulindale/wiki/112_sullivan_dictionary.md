- **[Sullivan1985]** Sullivan, D. (1985). *Quasiconformal homeomorphisms and
  dynamics I: Solution of the Fatou–Julia problem on wandering domains.* Ann.
  Math. 122(3), 401–418. — and the surrounding programme: **"Sullivan's
  dictionary"**, the systematic correspondence between **Kleinian groups**
  (discrete Möbius groups, limit sets) and **rational maps** (Julia sets),
  entry by entry (no-wandering-domains ↔ Ahlfors finiteness, etc.).
- **[Patterson1976]** Patterson, S. J. (1976). *The limit set of a Fuchsian
  group.* Acta Math. 136, 245–273. **[Sullivan1979]** Sullivan, D. (1979).
  *The density at infinity of a discrete group of hyperbolic motions.* Publ.
  IHÉS 50, 171–202. — the **Patterson–Sullivan measure**; the critical
  exponent δ that is at once an orbit-counting abscissa, a conformal-density
  exponent, and (via `λ₀ = δ(2−δ)`) the bottom of a Laplace spectrum.

## What it anchors

The engineered claim that **the Smith chart and the Apollonian gasket — and GR
and QM, and the continuous and the discrete — are two representations of one
object**, related by a fold, not two competing theories. "One loop through the
Möbius group"; "foldings of the circle"; the σ‑facet ladder of `Ĥ_RB`.
