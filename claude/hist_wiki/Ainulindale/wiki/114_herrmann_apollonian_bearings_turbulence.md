- **[HerrmannManticaBessis1990]** Herrmann, H. J., Mantica, G. & Bessis, D.
  (1990). *Space-filling bearings.* Phys. Rev. Lett. 65(26), 3223–3226. —
  Apollonian-type packings in which every disk can rotate **without slipping**
  at every contact; proposed as a model for **low-dissipation hierarchical
  shear transmission** in turbulent boundary layers and in fault gouge.
- **[FrischParisi1985]** Parisi, G. & Frisch, U. (1985). *On the singularity
  structure of fully developed turbulence.* In *Turbulence and Predictability
  in Geophysical Fluid Dynamics* (Varenna), 84–87. — the **multifractal
  model**: turbulent dissipation concentrates on a *spectrum* of interwoven
  fractal sets.
- **[FrischSulemNelkin1978]** Frisch, U., Sulem, P.-L. & Nelkin, M. (1978).
  *A simple dynamical model of intermittent fully developed turbulence.*
  J. Fluid Mech. 87, 719–736. — the **β-model**: the active eddies live on a
  recursively-thinned Cantor set in space.
- **[Richardson1922]** Richardson, L. F. (1922). *Weather Prediction by
  Numerical Process.* CUP. — "big whorls have little whorls…": the cascade
  as recursive gap-filling.
