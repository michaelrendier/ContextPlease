## Findings
1. T on each branch as ASS: even = SCALE(1/2); odd = ADD(1/2) @ SCALE(3/2). The engine's SIGN (±1, `ASS.SIGN`) occurs in NEITHER branch. The paper's "SIGN" is the parity-bit SELECTOR. Composing the branch elements along the true parity word reproduces T^k on every class exactly (8190 classes, k<=12, scale = 3^d/2^k exactly): MEASURED. Labelling issue (paper): T = ADD o SCALE o SIGN conflates the selector with ASS.SIGN. CAMSHAFT order SIGN,SCALE,ADD matches the branch order (select, scale, add).
2. Q_k (parity vector mod 2^k) is a permutation of Z/2^k, an isometry level by level (0 violations, k<=14), every cycle length a power of two, order 2^(k-4) for k>=6, sign -1 for k>=3: all KNOWN (Bernstein-Lagarias 1996: "order 2^(n-4) for n>=6", cycles powers of 2). Odd fixed-point counts per level reproduce their Table 2.2 column j=0 for n=2..20 EXACTLY (2,2,4,6,6,8,14,14,10,12,16,26,22,18,20,18,12,16,26) and Table 2.3/2.4 at n=21 (32), 30 (50), 40 (92), 100 (90), 160 (278), 200 (404). Extended by me only in depth they did not tabulate (n<=200 computed here; they reach 1000). -1 and 1/3 are fixed at every level tested, consistent with their conjecture of exactly two odd fixed points of Phi; levels do not separate them from the other odd residues (404 odd residues at n=200).
3. Long-Path memory (Recaman set-membership rule) on orbits of 27, 97, 871, 6171, 77031: the first repeated value is always 2, one step after the orbit reaches 1 (the {1,2} loop). The conjecture is equivalent to "the first collision is always the {1,2} loop (or there is none)".
4. Controls (CONTROL: 40 random bit strings of equal length): the parity streams of the five orbits are indistinguishable from random under linear complexity (L ~ N/2) and Re-Pair ratio; Lyndon-factor count was higher for all five (8-10 vs 5-7) from only five samples, unexplained, NOT a result.
