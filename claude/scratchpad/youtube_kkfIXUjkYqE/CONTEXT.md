Units can be multiplied, divided and cancelled; four increasingly cursed examples. (1) gas mileage: L/100 km = 0.01 mm² (a cross-section), mpg likewise; (2) kilowatt-hour = power x time = 3.6 MJ ("multiplying 60 km/h by 20 minutes"); G has units m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻², dimensional analysis can even find the equation; (3) Hubble constant km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ = 2.3e-18 Hz, its inverse about 14 Gyr against the true 13.7 Gyr (a coincidence; the constant is not constant in time); (4) fractional exponents: polarisation mode dispersion ps/√km (random walk: dispersion ∝ √length), scattering measure pc·m^(-20/3), nm/√nm. Take-away used in Equation Space: unit exponents are rational, not only integer; the unit stays in the form that describes how it was measured.
