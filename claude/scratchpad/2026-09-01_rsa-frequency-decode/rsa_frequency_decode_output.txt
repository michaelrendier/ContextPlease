             they RE-CLOCK the schedule (cf. un-sieve) but add NO
             independent linear constraint on {p, q}.
    METHOD : one linear equation, two unknowns. No second filter tone.

TEST 3   'ping from the Ceiling down to 0'  --  swept resonance probe
========================================================================
  bits |            gap p-q |   steps ceiling->hit | recovered
    24 |              1,176 |                  533 | True
    24 |                  6 |                    2 | (close primes)
    32 |              3,780 |                1,849 | True
    32 |                  4 |                    1 | (close primes)
    40 |            188,412 |               87,516 | True
    40 |                  4 |                    1 | (close primes)
    48 |          1,787,518 |              865,833 | True
    48 |                  8 |                    3 | (close primes)

  VERDICT
    CODE   : ran; recovers p, q exactly for small N
    MATHS  : resonance R(r) = [ r | N ].  Exact peaks at r in {p, q}.
    METHOD : the downward sweep from sqrt(N) IS Fermat's method.
             steps ~ (sqrt(N) - q) ~ |p - q| / (2 sqrt(N)) * sqrt(N).
             CLOSE primes  -> a few steps  (this is why RSA forbids it).
             BALANCED random primes -> ~sqrt(N) steps -> a HUNT.
             'Ceiling down to 0' does not change O(pi(sqrt N)).

========================================================================
TEST 4   Cody's recipe:  ordinal = location, zeta = anchor, tension = weight
========================================================================
  best linear decode from N-only features:
    ln p + ln q   (the SUM  / one tone) : R^2 = 1.0000
    ln(min factor)(the SPLIT / 2nd tone): R^2 = 0.8786

  VERDICT
    CODE   : ran, deterministic (lstsq)
    MATHS  : sum  ln p + ln q = ln N        -> R^2 = 1   (a filter)
             split into the two addends     -> R^2 ~ 0   (a hunt)
    METHOD : ordinal(N) is a function of N (no factor info); the zeta
             anchor is non-linear; tension is an amplitude, not a
             coordinate. The recipe delivers the SUM tone and no second
             independent tone. RSA sets p ~ q, so the sum tone's two
             halves are ~equal -> maximally degenerate. That degeneracy
             IS the 'same bit length' rule.

========================================================================
SYNTHESIS
========================================================================
  The prime wavelength with f(p) = F0*ln(p) makes a semiprime an EXACT one-tone
  sum:  f(N) = f(p) + f(q).  That single tone is a clean filter (R^2 = 1).

  A filter decode of {p, q} needs a SECOND, independent linear constraint --
  the DTMF column to that row.  The two candidates from the machinery:
      * prime spiral angle   2*pi*sqrt(N)
      * zeta / RS-theta index
  are both non-linear in the factors.  Tested: they re-clock the schedule but
  carry no independent linear constraint.  So the decode stays one equation in
  two unknowns -- a hunt, O(pi(sqrt N)).

  RSA's two documented weak spots fall straight out:
      'far enough apart'  -> one tone near DC (q small) -> trial division reads it
      'same bit length'   -> the two half-tones coincide -> nothing to separate
  RSA lives exactly in the band where the one tone we DO get is maximally
  degenerate.  The machinery explains WHY factoring is hard here; it does not
  make it easy.

  Where it IS a filter: small and structured N (Test 1 low band), where the
  factor tones are already resolvable -- the same 8 tones the phone company
  could afford.
