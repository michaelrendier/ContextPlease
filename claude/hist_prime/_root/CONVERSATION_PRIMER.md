**Coordinate system (locked, consistent across SCAD + Three.js + FITS WCS):**
- x = East      (RA decreasing → East positive)
- y = North     (DEC increasing → North positive)
- z = toward observer   (+LOS, integration direction for Faraday rotation)
- 1 unit = 100 kpc  (1 arcmin ≈ 2.06 units at z=0.296)

**Why "up and down" matters for FITS processing:**
When loading Q/U frequency cubes: axis1=RA (→-x), axis2=DEC (→y), axis3=frequency.

Every FITS pixel (i,j) maps to (x,y) in this coordinate system.
The LOS column at DM_SE shows WHY the projection correction (note 10) matters:
the column samples gas at ALL z, not just z where the DM actually sits.
